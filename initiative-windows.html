Seniors are getting their windows fully replaced thanks to this initiative

February 18, 2026

A new Senior Home Improvement Grant for Windows is providing comprehensive window replacement services for eligible seniors.

By Margaret Whitfield, Staff Writer 5 min read

A new federally backed initiative is helping seniors across the country fully replace their old, drafty windows — often at little or no out-of-pocket cost — in an effort to lower monthly utility bills and help older Americans age safely in place.

The program covers energy-focused upgrades including ENERGY STAR-certified double-pane windows, Low-E glass coatings, weather-tight seals, and easy-open tilt-in sashes. Eligible homeowners are matched with vetted local contractors, with most approved files moving forward within just a few business days.

“My 63-year-old husband and I were nervous about the cost. The match took two minutes and we had brand new windows in every room within weeks — and our heating bill dropped right away.” — Linda K., approved homeowner

Program reviewers say many seniors are surprised to learn they qualify. Eligibility is typically based on age, homeownership status, and basic location details — not income alone.

Why now?

Old single-pane windows are responsible for 25 to 30 percent of a home's heating and cooling energy loss, according to the Department of Energy. By making it easier and more affordable to replace them, this initiative aims to keep more seniors comfortable in their homes longer and reduce avoidable utility costs on fixed incomes.

Who qualifies?

The match check takes about two minutes online. Homeowners aged 60 and older are encouraged to confirm a few basic details about their home to see if they're eligible for the program in their state.
