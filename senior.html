Senior Bathroom Assistance 2025

A new wave of bathroom safety programs is making it easier for seniors across the country to upgrade outdated tubs and showers into safer, step-free spaces — often at little or no out-of-pocket cost.

Falls in the bathroom remain one of the leading causes of injury for adults 60 and older. In response, a growing number of state programs, veteran benefits, and home-safety funds are now covering walk-in showers, grab bars, raised toilets, and non-slip flooring for qualifying homeowners.

Approved upgrades may include:
  • Step-free walk-in showers with bench seating
  • Grab bars and reinforced safety rails
  • Comfort-height toilets and raised vanities
  • Non-slip flooring and improved lighting

Program reviewers say many seniors are surprised to learn they qualify. Eligibility is typically based on age, homeownership, and basic location details — not income alone. The match check takes about two minutes online.

How seniors are qualifying

Most approved homeowners complete a short eligibility form, confirm a few basic details about their home, and are then matched with the right program in their area. Reviewers note that homes built before 1995 are especially likely to be approved for upgrades.
