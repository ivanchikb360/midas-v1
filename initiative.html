Seniors are getting their bathrooms fully remodeled thanks to this initiative

September 24, 2025

The Social Security Administration announces a new initiative providing comprehensive bathroom remodeling services for eligible seniors.

By Margaret Whitfield, Staff Writer 5 min read

A new federally backed initiative is helping seniors across the country fully remodel their bathrooms — often at little or no out-of-pocket cost — in an effort to reduce home injuries and help older Americans age safely in place.

The program covers safety-focused upgrades including step-free walk-in showers, grab bars, comfort-height toilets, and non-slip flooring. Eligible homeowners are matched with vetted local contractors, with most approved files moving forward within just a few business days.

“My 63-year-old husband and I were nervous about the cost. The match took two minutes and we had a brand new walk-in shower within weeks.” — Linda K., approved homeowner

Program reviewers say many seniors are surprised to learn they qualify. Eligibility is typically based on age, homeownership status, and basic location details — not income alone.

Why now?

Falls in the bathroom remain one of the leading causes of injury for adults 60 and older. By making it easier and more affordable to install safer fixtures, this initiative aims to keep more seniors in their homes longer and out of long-term care facilities.

Who qualifies?

The match check takes about two minutes online. Homeowners aged 60 and older are encouraged to confirm a few basic details about their home to see if they're eligible for the program in their state.
